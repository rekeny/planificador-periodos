## Purpose and principles

The app plans which periods each player plays in youth basketball matches and warns about possible breaches of the rules of each format. Planning is done before the match and adjusted freely during it, without saving versions.

Principles of the constitution that weigh most here:

- **1 · Inform, never block.** Any combination of marks can be made even if it breaks the rules. The only limit is that a period never has more than 5 X. The decision always belongs to the coach.
- **2 · Nothing changes by itself.** The app never changes marks on its own. Marks are only deleted when the coach confirms it.
- **3 and 5 · Offline, no data leaving, children's data.** Everything is stored on the device, with no accounts and no sync, and the data is kept out of device backups (RF-088).
- **4 · Every tap is saved at once.** Not stated in the source; made explicit by clarification in RF-082: grid changes and confirmed actions are saved at once, forms with «Guardar».
- **6 · Simple.** Little data and few taps.
- **7 · In Spanish.** User-facing texts are quoted in Spanish between «», with an English gloss in brackets.

## Scope

**In**

- An app for iOS and Android, on phone and tablet, in portrait and landscape, that works offline and in Spanish.
- Teams, their players and their matches, stored only on the device. Someone using two devices enters the data on each one.
- The call-up of each match.
- The match grid: marks, undo and overtimes.
- Warnings, colours and the player counter.
- The lists of teams and matches, deletions and confirmations.

**Out**

- Automatic planning proposals and substitution recommendations.
- "Can play" colours (green).
- Statistics and season summaries.
- Sharing, exporting and backups.
- Marking injuries or players fouled out.
- Changing a player's number for a specific match.
- Players shared between teams: they are added to each team separately.
- Custom period configurations.
- Other languages.
- Accounts and sync between devices.
- Any link with the scoreboard app: this app is independent of it.
- Publishing in the stores (the intended use is personal at first; publishing is a long-term goal).

## User stories

The priorities are the ones proposed in the source, confirmed by Fernando on 2026-11-11 (see Clarifications). With the P1 stories the app can already be used in a match.

### US1 · Create teams and players (P1)

As a coach, I want to save my teams with their players so I don't have to enter them for every match.

Acceptance criteria:

1. When I create a team with a name, category and default format, it appears in the list of teams.
2. When I add a player with a name and number, they appear in the team, sorted by number, with "0" before "00".
3. If there is already a player with number 7 and I add another one with 7, it is saved and the duplicate-number warning appears.
4. A number such as "07" cannot be saved, and the message tells me why; a name field does not let me type more than 40 characters.
5. If the app closes while I am filling in a form, or I leave the form without «Guardar», what I had not saved is lost, without the app asking; everything saved before is kept.
6. The names I enter are not copied into the device backups.

Requirements: RF-001, RF-011, RF-012, RF-013, RF-014, RF-016, RF-017, RF-069, RF-073, RF-082, RF-085, RF-088.

### US2 · Create a match and its call-up (P1)

As a coach, I want to create a match with the players who are going to play.

Acceptance criteria:

1. In a team whose default format is 6 periods, the new match has 6 periods, today's date and no time.
2. With 12 active players, all 12 are in the call-up when the match is created, and I can deselect those who are not playing.
3. In a 6-period match with 8 players in the call-up, the call-up-outside-the-range warning appears.
4. If I create a player from the match, they stay in the team and in the call-up.
5. In the new-match form I see the format filled with the team default and I can change it without any confirmation.
6. I pick the date and the time with the device pickers; the date is shown as «01/03/2026» and the time as «18:30», and I can clear a chosen time with «Quitar hora».

### US3 · Plan the periods (P1)

As a coach, I want to mark who starts each period.

Acceptance criteria:

1. In a period with fewer than 5 X, tapping an empty cell marks an X.
2. In a period with no subs, tapping an X removes it.
3. Pressing Undo several times reverts the last actions, one by one.
4. Adding an overtime shows a new column, which can be removed.
5. Undoing a mark whose player or column is no longer in the grid skips that cell and undoes the rest; if none of its cells is left, the same press undoes the previous action.
6. If a mark cannot be saved, the cell stays as it was and the app tells me.

### US4 · See the rule warnings (P1)

As a coach, I want to see immediately whether the plan breaks the rules.

4. If the rule can no longer be met with the empty cells left in the rule window, the general warning appears.

### US5 · Record subs during the match (P2)

As a coach, I want to record substitutions during a period without messing up the numbers.

### 1 · Rules

- **RF-001** · There are three formats: 4 periods, 6 periods and 8 periods. Criterion: wherever a format is chosen (team default format, match format), exactly these three options are offered. Stories: US1, US2, US6.
- **RF-002** · Each format has a rule window made of its first periods: P1–P3 in 4 periods, P1–P5 in 6 periods and P1–P6 in 8 periods. Criterion: rule-window warnings are evaluated only on those periods for each format (E-001, E-008, E-009). Stories: US4.
- **RF-003** · The minimum number of periods played in the rule window is 1 in 4 periods, 2 in 6 periods and 2 in 8 periods. Criterion: the too-few-periods-played warning uses these minimums (E-004). Stories: US4.
- **RF-004** · The minimum number of periods rested in the rule window is 1 in 4 periods, 2 in 6 periods and 2 in 8 periods. Criterion: the light red cell and the not-enough-rest warning use these minimums (E-001, E-003, E-008, E-009). Stories: US4.
- **RF-005** · The allowed number of players in the call-up is 8–12 in 4 periods, 9–12 in 6 periods and 8–12 in 8 periods. Criterion: the call-up-outside-the-range warning uses these limits (E-015). Stories: US2.
- **RF-006** · The periods after the rule window and all overtimes are free periods: no rule applies to them and they are never coloured. Criterion: in 8 periods P7 and P8 never get a colour (E-008); in 4 periods P4 never gets a colour (E-009); an overtime never gets a colour. Stories: US4.
- **RF-007** · A period counts as played for a player when their cell has an X (the player starts the period), even if they leave the court during it. Criterion: each X adds one played period to the counter and to the rule-window evaluation (E-004, E-014). Stories: US3, US4.
- **RF-008** · A period counts as rested for a player when their cell is empty (the player does not step on court during the whole period). Criterion: each empty cell adds one rested period to the counter and to the rule-window evaluation (E-001, E-014). Stories: US3, US4.
- **RF-009** · A circled X (the player comes on as a sub during the period, even for one second) counts neither as played nor as rested. Criterion: a circled X adds nothing to played or rested, in the counter or in the rule window (E-002, E-010, E-014). Stories: US4, US5.
- **RF-010** · A call-up with fewer players than the minimum of the format, or with more than 12, is never blocked: the app only warns. Criterion: with 8 players in a 6-period match, and with 13 players in any format, the match can be created and its grid can be marked, and the call-up warning is shown (E-015, E-030). Stories: US2.

### 2 · Data: teams, players and matches

- **RF-011** · All data (teams, players and matches) is stored only on the device; the app works without a network connection, without accounts and without sync. Criterion: with no network connection every story can be completed, and there is no sign-in step. Stories: US1, US2, US3, US4, US5, US6, US7.
- **RF-012** · The interface is only in Spanish. Criterion: every text the user sees is in Spanish and there is no language option. Stories: US1, US2, US3, US4, US5, US6, US7.

#### Teams

- **RF-013** · The coach can create a team with a name, a category and a default format (4, 6 or 8 periods), and can later edit the three. The name is required (not blank) and has at most 40 characters; the category is optional free text of at most 40 characters. A field at its limit does not accept more text and shows no message. Criterion: after creating it, the team appears in the list of teams with those data; a team with a blank name cannot be saved; the name and category fields do not accept a 41st character (E-022); after editing its name or category, the list shows the new data. Stories: US1.
- **RF-014** · Several teams can exist at the same time. Criterion: after creating two teams, both appear in the list of teams. Stories: US1.
- **RF-015** · Deleting a team asks for a confirmation that states what will be deleted; on confirming, the team, its players and its matches are deleted. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: after confirming, neither the team, nor its players, nor its matches appear anywhere; after cancelling, nothing changes; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and changes nothing. Stories: US7.

#### Players

- **RF-016** · The coach can add a player to a team with a name and a number. The team's players are shown sorted by the numeric value of the number, with "0" before "00", and by name when two players share a number. The name is required (not blank) and has at most 40 characters; the name field at its limit does not accept more text and shows no message. The number is required and is one of 0–99 written without a leading zero, or "00": "0" and "00" are different numbers, and a two-digit number starting with 0, such as "07", is not valid. A missing or invalid number shows «El dorsal debe ser de 0 a 99 o 00, sin ceros a la izquierda.» [The number must be from 0 to 99 or 00, without leading zeros.]. Criterion: after adding players 9, 4 and 7, the team shows them in the order 4, 7, 9; two players with number 7, Luis and Ana, are shown Ana first; E-020; E-021; a player with a blank name or without a number cannot be saved; the name field does not accept a 41st character (E-022). Stories: US1.
- **RF-017** · A player can be saved with the same number as another player of the same team. Criterion: adding a second player with number 7 saves them (and the duplicate-number warning of RF-069 appears). Stories: US1.
- **RF-018** · The coach can change a player's name and number in the team, with the same validation as RF-016. Criterion: after the change, the team shows the new name and number; changing the number to "07" cannot be saved. Stories: US7.
- **RF-019** · Changing a player's name or number does not change matches already created: they keep the data the player had; matches created afterwards use the new data. A match keeps the name and number the player had when they entered its call-up, so a player added to an older match after the change shows the new data in it. Criterion: E-018; after changing Juan from 7 to 9 and adding him to the call-up of a match created before the change, that match shows 9. Stories: US7.
- **RF-020** · A player belongs to a single team; a person who plays in two teams is added to each team as a separate player. Criterion: the same name and number can be added to two teams, and changing it in one team does not change it in the other. Stories: US7.
- **RF-021** · Players are never deleted individually: they only disappear when their team is deleted. Criterion: there is no action to delete a player; deleting the team removes them (RF-015). Stories: US7.
- **RF-022** · Deactivating a player asks for confirmation; on confirming, the player is deactivated. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: after confirming, the player is shown as deactivated in the team; after cancelling, they stay active; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and the player active. Stories: US7.
- **RF-023** · A deactivated player does not appear in new call-ups and is not offered when adding players to a call-up, but remains, with their marks, in the matches already saved. Criterion: after deactivating Ana, a new match does not include her and she cannot be added to it; a saved match that included her still shows her and her marks. Stories: US7.
- **RF-024** · A deactivated player can be reactivated, without confirmation, and then appears again in new call-ups. Criterion: reactivating Ana asks nothing; after reactivating her, a new match includes her in the call-up. Stories: US7.

#### Matches

- **RF-025** · Each match belongs to a team and holds a format, a date, a time, a home team name, an away team name, an optional final score, notes and its added overtimes, together with its call-up and its grid. Only the format and the date are required (both have a default); a new match starts with no time. The time, the home and away names, the final score and the notes are optional. The home and away names have at most 40 characters each and the notes at most 500. The final score is two whole numbers from 0 to 999, home and away, entered together or not at all. A field at its limit (40 or 500 characters, or three digits in a score) does not accept more text and shows no message. The date and the time are entered as in RF-083. Criterion: all these data can be entered, and they are shown unchanged when the match is opened again; a match with only the format and the date can be saved; the new-match form starts with no time chosen, and a match created without choosing one has no time; E-022. Stories: US2, US6.
- **RF-026** · The date of a new match is today by default and can be changed. Criterion: a match created on 2026-11-11 shows that date; it can be changed to another date and keeps it. Stories: US2.
- **RF-027** · The home and away team names are free text, with a quick option to put the coach's own team as home or as away. The quick option writes only the team name (without the category), and the text can then be edited. Criterion: any text can be typed in each field; using the quick option fills the chosen field with the team name. Stories: US2.
- **RF-028** · A saved match can be edited: its details and its grid. Criterion: after changing a detail or a mark of a saved match and opening it again, the change is there. Stories: US6.
- **RF-029** · Deleting a match asks for confirmation; on confirming, the match and its grid are deleted. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: after confirming, the match no longer appears in any list; after cancelling, nothing changes; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and changes nothing. Stories: US6.

### 3 · Call-up

- **RF-030** · When a match is created, all the active players of the team are selected in its call-up, and no deactivated player is. Criterion: in a team with 12 active players and 1 deactivated, the new match starts with exactly the 12 active ones. Stories: US2.
- **RF-031** · When creating the match, the coach can deselect the players who are not playing; this only affects that match. Criterion: a player deselected in one match is still in the team and is selected by default in the next new match. Stories: US2.
- **RF-032** · Players can be added to the call-up of an existing match, and only active players of the team are offered. Criterion: when adding, the deactivated players of the team are not offered; the added player appears in the grid. Stories: US2.
- **RF-033** · Players can be removed from the call-up of an existing match; a player with no marked cells is removed without confirmation. Criterion: after removing a player, their row no longer appears in that match's grid; removing a player with no marks asks nothing. Stories: US2.
- **RF-034** · Removing from the call-up a player who already has marked cells asks for confirmation; on confirming, their marks in that match are deleted. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: E-017; after cancelling, the player and their marks stay; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and changes nothing. Stories: US2.
- **RF-035** · A new player can be created from the match: they are added to the team and to the call-up. Criterion: the new player appears in the team and in the grid of that match. Stories: US2.

### 4 · Match format

- **RF-036** · A new match takes the team's default format, which the coach can change in the new-match form before creating it (RF-084). Criterion: in a team whose default format is 6 periods, a match created without touching the format has 6 periods. Stories: US2.
- **RF-037** · Changing a team's default format only affects matches created afterwards. Criterion: after changing the default from 6 to 8 periods, existing matches keep their format and a new match has 8 periods. Stories: US2.
- **RF-038** · The format of a match can be changed to any of the three formats at any time, before or during the match. Criterion: a match can be changed between 4, 6 and 8 periods in any direction. Stories: US6.
- **RF-039** · Changing the format of a match always shows a general warning that information may be lost and asks for confirmation; on cancelling, nothing changes. Choosing the format the match already has is not a change: no warning or confirmation is shown and nothing changes. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: the warning and the confirmation appear on every format change, even when no mark would be lost (E-016); choosing 6 periods for a 6-period match shows no warning or confirmation and leaves its marks as they were; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and changes nothing. Stories: US6.
- **RF-040** · On confirming a format change, the marks in the periods that still exist are kept and the marks in the periods that disappear are deleted. Criterion: E-016; changing from 8 to 4 periods keeps the marks of P1–P4. Stories: US6.
- **RF-041** · On a format change, the added overtimes are kept, with their marks, after the new last period. Criterion: a 6-period match with 1 overtime changed to 4 periods shows P1–P4 followed by the overtime with its marks. Stories: US6.

### 5 · Grid and marking

- **RF-042** · The grid has one row per player in the call-up, sorted as in RF-016 (by number, "0" before "00", and by name when two players share a number), and one column per period of the format followed by one column per added overtime. Criterion: a 6-period match with 1 overtime and 10 players shows 10 rows sorted by number and 7 columns. Stories: US3.
- **RF-043** · Each cell is in one of three states: empty (rested), X (starts the period) or circled X (comes on as a sub). Criterion: every cell always shows exactly one of the three states. Stories: US3.
- **RF-044** · Tapping an empty cell in a period with fewer than 5 X marks an X. Criterion: in a period with 4 X, tapping an empty cell leaves it with an X and the period with 5 X. Stories: US3.
- **RF-045** · Tapping an empty cell in a rule-window period that already has 5 X asks «¿Entra de cambio o es titular en lugar de otro?» [Is this player coming on as a sub, or starting in place of someone else?], with the options sub and starter, and it can be cancelled. Cancelling, either here or while choosing which X is replaced (RF-047, RF-053), leaves the grid as it was: the tapped cell stays empty. Tapping outside the question or the choice, or pressing the Android Back button while it is open, cancels it in the same way. Criterion: E-011; after cancelling the question or the choice of the replaced X, with its cancel option, by tapping outside it or by pressing Back on Android, no cell has changed. Stories: US5.
- **RF-046** · Answering sub to the question of RF-045 marks a circled X in the tapped cell. Criterion: after answering sub, the cell shows a circled X and the period still has 5 X. Stories: US5.
- **RF-047** · Answering starter to the question of RF-045 lets the coach choose which X it replaces; the tapped cell gets an X and the chosen player's X is removed, leaving that cell empty (not a circled X), both in a single step. Criterion: after choosing Eva, the tapped cell has an X, Eva's cell is empty and the period still has 5 X. Stories: US5.
- **RF-048** · Tapping an empty cell in a free period or an overtime that already has 5 X marks a circled X without asking. Criterion: E-012. Stories: US5.
- **RF-049** · Tapping an X in a period with no sub removes the X. Criterion: in a period with 5 X and no circled X, tapping an X leaves the cell empty and the period with 4 X. Stories: US3.
- **RF-050** · Tapping an X in a period with some sub shows the reminder «Si ha salido por un cambio, déjala marcada: sigue contando como jugado» [If they left the court for a sub, keep it marked: it still counts as played], with the options remove anyway and cancel; tapping outside the reminder, or pressing the Android Back button while it is open, cancels it. Criterion: E-013; on cancelling, with the cancel option, by tapping outside or by pressing Back on Android, the X stays. Stories: US5.
- **RF-051** · Tapping a circled X removes it, in any period. Criterion: after tapping a circled X, the cell is empty. Stories: US5.
- **RF-052** · A long press on a marked cell (X or circled X) opens the menu «Titular» [Starter], «Entró de cambio» [Came on as a sub], «Quitar» [Remove], which changes the cell to X, to circled X or to empty, respectively. The menu always shows the three options; choosing the one that matches the current state of the cell changes nothing. Choosing «Quitar» on an X in a period with some sub shows the reminder of RF-050 first, as a tap does. A long press on an empty cell does nothing. Tapping outside the menu, or pressing the Android Back button while it is open, closes it and changes nothing. Criterion: a long press on a circled X and choosing «Titular» in a period with 4 X leaves an X; choosing «Entró de cambio» on an X leaves a circled X; choosing «Quitar» leaves the cell empty; choosing «Quitar» on an X in a period with a sub shows the reminder; a long press on an empty cell neither marks it nor opens a menu; tapping outside the open menu, or pressing Back on Android, leaves the cell as it was. Stories: US5.
- **RF-053** · Choosing «Titular» [Starter] in the long-press menu when the period already has 5 X asks which X it replaces, as in RF-047. Criterion: in a period with 5 X, turning a circled X into «Titular» asks which X it replaces and the period still has 5 X. Stories: US5.
- **RF-054** · The app never changes marks on its own: when an X is removed, no circled X becomes an X automatically. Criterion: in E-013, after removing Eva's X the sub stays as a circled X and the period has 4 X. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-055** · A period can never have more than 5 X; this is the only limit on marking. Criterion: no sequence of taps, long presses, format changes or undos leaves any period with 6 X or more. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-056** · The grid can be edited freely before and during the match, and no versions of the plan are saved. Criterion: marks can be changed at any moment and only the current state of the grid is kept. Stories: US3, US6.

### 6 · Undo and overtimes

- **RF-057** · An Undo button undoes the last action, and it can be pressed several times in a row to undo earlier actions, one by one. Undo only covers changes of marks in the grid: taps, long-press menu choices, "remove anyway" and the starter replacement, which changes two cells and is undone in a single press. It does not cover call-up changes, added or removed overtimes, format changes or match details. The history belongs to the open match and is lost when leaving it or when the app closes (RF-082). Cells that no longer exist are skipped, and an action none of whose cells exists is discarded (RF-086). Criterion: after marking three cells and pressing Undo three times, the grid is as before the first mark; after a starter replacement, one press restores both cells; adding an overtime and pressing Undo does not remove it. Stories: US3.
- **RF-058** · The «Añadir prórroga» [Add overtime] button adds an overtime column after the last column, and as many overtimes as needed can be added. Criterion: pressing it twice in a 6-period match shows 8 columns. Stories: US3.
- **RF-059** · Only the last added overtime can be removed. If it has marks, removing it asks for confirmation and, on confirming, its marks are deleted (constitution principle 2); if it has none, it is removed without asking. The confirmation closes only with its buttons: tapping outside it, or pressing the Android Back button, does nothing. Criterion: after removing the only overtime of a 6-period match, the grid shows 6 columns; with two overtimes, only the second one offers removal; removing an overtime with marks asks for confirmation, and after cancelling it stays with its marks; tapping outside the open confirmation, or pressing Back on Android, leaves it open and the overtime with its marks. Stories: US3.

### 7 · Warnings and colours

- **RF-060** · Warnings are recalculated on every tap and never prevent marking. Criterion: after each change of the grid, the call-up or the format, the warnings shown match the new state, and every mark that does not create a sixth X is made even if it breaks a rule. Stories: US4.
- **RF-061** · By default everything is white: there is no green colour and there are no recommendations. Criterion: a grid with no warnings has no coloured cell or player, and the app never suggests who should play. Stories: US4.
- **RF-062** · A player's possible rests in the rule window are their empty cells in the window, whether or not those periods are complete yet. An empty rule-window cell of a player whose possible rests are at or below the minimum (so that marking it would leave them below it) shows a light red background. Criterion: E-001, E-002, E-003, E-008, E-009, E-010; in E-003 P5 is light red although the possible rests are already below the minimum. Stories: US4.
- **RF-063** · A player whose possible rests in the rule window (RF-062) are below the minimum is shown in red, immediately. Criterion: E-003; for example 4 X in the first 5 periods of a 6-period match. Stories: US4.
- **RF-064** · A player who is missing periods played in the rule window is shown in red only when every period of the window already has 5 X; while any period of the window has fewer than 5 X, it is not shown. The red is the same as in RF-063: the app does not tell which of the two warnings it is. Criterion: E-004 and E-005; a player breaking both rules looks the same as one breaking only one. Stories: US4.
- **RF-065** · A rule-window period with fewer than 5 X (incomplete period) shows «!» next to the period name in its column header. Free periods and overtimes never show it. Criterion: E-007; a window period with 4 X shows «!» and one with 5 X does not; an empty free period or overtime does not show it. Stories: US4.
- **RF-066** · A period with a sub and fewer than 5 X (inconsistent period), in any column (rule window, free period or overtime), shows «≠» next to the period name in its column header. When a rule-window period is both incomplete and inconsistent, only «≠» is shown. Criterion: E-013, E-002, E-010; after E-013, P3 shows «≠» and not «!»; P6 with 4 X and a sub shows «≠». Stories: US5.
- **RF-067** · A general warning is shown above the grid when there is no possible filling of the rule window: no way to add X in the empty cells of the window, keeping every existing X and circled X as it is, so that every window period has exactly 5 X and every player in the call-up meets both the minimum played (RF-003) and the minimum rested (RF-004). It is shown together with any player in red, and also when every period of the window already has 5 X but some player breaks a minimum. Criterion: E-006, E-003, E-004, E-015 and E-030 show it; E-001, E-002, E-005, E-007, E-008, E-009 and E-010 do not. Stories: US4.
- **RF-068** · When the number of players in the call-up is below the minimum of the format or above 12, a warning is shown both while selecting the call-up when creating the match and in the match screen. Criterion: E-015; with 9 or 12 players in a 6-period match it is not shown, with 8 or 13 it is, in the call-up selection and in the match. Stories: US2.
- **RF-069** · When two active players of the same team have the same number, a warning is shown in the team; deactivated players do not count. Criterion: with two active players with number 7 the warning is shown; after changing one of them to 8, or deactivating one of them, it disappears. Stories: US1.

### 8 · Player counter

- **RF-070** · Each player shows a counter of the periods played and rested over the whole match, overtimes included, in the form «J 3 · D 3» [P 3 · R 3: played 3, rested 3]. Criterion: E-014. Stories: US3.
- **RF-071** · A sub adds neither to played nor to rested in the counter, so played plus rested can be lower than the number of columns. Criterion: E-014; a player with one circled X in a 6-period match with no overtime has played plus rested equal to 5. Stories: US3.
- **RF-072** · Colours and player warnings only look at the rule window, while the counter looks at the whole match. Criterion: marks in free periods and overtimes change the counter but never the light red cells or the player in red (E-008, E-014). Stories: US4.

### 9 · Lists, deletions and confirmations

- **RF-073** · When the app opens, the list of teams is shown, sorted alphabetically by name. Criterion: opening the app shows the list of teams first; teams «Cadete B» and «Alevín A» are shown «Alevín A» first. Stories: US1.
- **RF-074** · There is a list with every match of every team, sorted by date with the most recent first; on the same date, by time with the latest first, and matches without a time after those with one. Each match shows its date (as in RF-083), time, home and away names, final score (if any) and format; only the list with every match also shows the team name and category. Criterion: with matches of two teams on 2026-11-01, 2026-11-08 and 2026-11-15, the list shows all of them in the order 15, 8, 1; two matches on 2026-11-08 at 10:00 and 12:00 are shown 12:00 first. Stories: US6.
- **RF-075** · Each team has a list of its own matches, sorted and shown as in RF-074, without the team name and category. Criterion: the list of a team shows only its matches, the most recent first, each with its format and without the team name and category. Stories: US6.

### 10 · Phone and tablet

- **RF-076** · The app works in portrait and landscape, on phone and on tablet. Criterion: every story can be completed on a phone and on a tablet, in both orientations. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-077** · On a phone and a tablet, in portrait and in landscape, the player column stays fixed, showing the number and name of each player with the counter below. Criterion: when the grid scrolls, the player column with number, name and counter stays visible, in every device and orientation. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-078** · Grid cells are never narrower than 44 points; when the columns do not fit at that width, the grid scrolls horizontally. This applies to overtimes on every device and orientation. Criterion: on a phone in portrait, an 8-period match, and any match with overtimes that do not fit, scrolls horizontally with cells of at least 44 points. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-079** · On a phone in portrait, 4-period and 6-period matches without overtimes fit across without scrolling horizontally. The reference is the narrowest supported phone, 375 points wide in portrait. Criterion: on a 375-point-wide phone, a 4-period and a 6-period match without overtimes show every period without horizontal scroll. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-080** · On a phone in landscape, all periods of the format fit across; overtimes that do not fit scroll horizontally (RF-078); if the players do not fit, the grid scrolls vertically. Criterion: an 8-period match without overtimes shows every period without horizontal scroll; with 12 players that do not fit, the grid scrolls vertically. Stories: US3, US5.
- **RF-081** · On a tablet, the whole grid of the format fits in both orientations; overtimes that do not fit scroll horizontally (RF-078). Criterion: an 8-period match with 12 players and no overtimes shows every cell without scrolling, in portrait and in landscape. Stories: US3, US5.

### 11 · Saving

- **RF-082** · Every change of the grid (marks and overtimes) and every confirmed action outside a form (call-up changes in an existing match, deactivation and reactivation, deletions and the format change of a saved match, once confirmed) is saved on the device at once, without a save button: closing the app, or the app being closed by the system, loses none of them. Forms (team, player, new match with its call-up, and match details) are saved only with «Guardar» [Save]: a form that is half filled when the app closes is lost, and leaving a form without «Guardar» discards it without asking and saves nothing from it. When reopened, the app shows the list of teams (RF-073) and every team, player and match, with its grid, is exactly as it was after the last saved change; the undo history is lost (RF-057). Added by clarification from constitution principle 4. Criterion: E-019; E-029; going back from a half-filled new-team form shows no question and the team does not appear in the list of teams. Stories: US1, US2, US3, US6, US7.

### 12 · Details decided while planning

- **RF-083** · The date and the time of a match are chosen with the device's own date and time pickers, not typed as text. The date is shown as day/month/year with two-digit day and month, such as «01/03/2026», and the time in 24-hour format, such as «18:30», wherever a match date or time appears (match form and match lists). Because the time is optional, the match form offers «Quitar hora» [Clear time], which leaves the match without a time. Criterion: E-023; E-032; the match form has no field where the date or the time is typed as text. Stories: US2, US6.
- **RF-084** · The new-match form shows the format, filled with the team's default format (RF-036). The coach can change it there before creating the match, without any warning or confirmation, because no mark exists yet; the change does not alter the team's default format. Once the match is created, a format change follows RF-039. Criterion: E-024. Stories: US2.
- **RF-085** · If saving a change on the device fails (for example, because the device is full), the change is not applied: the saved data and what the app shows stay as they were before the change, Undo gets no new entry, and the message «No se ha podido guardar el cambio. Inténtalo de nuevo.» [The change could not be saved. Try again.] is shown. This applies to grid changes, confirmed actions and forms saved with «Guardar». Criterion: E-025; when saving a new team with «Guardar» fails, the team does not appear in the list of teams and the message is shown. Stories: US1, US2, US3, US5, US6, US7.
- **RF-086** · When Undo reverts an action that changed a cell that no longer exists (its overtime was removed, its period disappeared in a format change, or its player was removed from the call-up), that cell is skipped and the rest of the action's cells are reverted. When none of the action's cells exists any more, that history entry is discarded and the same press goes on to undo the previous action, with the same rule; if no entry is left, the press changes nothing. Undo never brings back a column or a call-up row. Criterion: E-026; E-031. Stories: US3.
- **RF-087** · A legend, shown under the general warning (RF-067), explains in Spanish the meaning of the warning marks visible in the grid at that moment, and only those: light red cell (RF-062), player in red (RF-063, RF-064), «!» (RF-065) and «≠» (RF-066). A mark counts as visible when it is in the grid, even if the coach has scrolled it out of view, so scrolling never changes the legend. When none of these marks is visible, there is no legend. Criterion: E-027; a grid with no warning shows no legend; after the last light red cell disappears, its meaning disappears from the legend; with the only light red cell scrolled out of view, the legend still explains it, and scrolling the grid does not change the legend. Stories: US4.
- **RF-088** · The app's data, which includes children's names, is kept out of device backups (constitution principle 5): on iOS it is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, and on Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled. Criterion: the app configuration versioned in the repository disables Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer, and marks the app's data as excluded from iOS backups, so the three settings can be checked by reading it; E-028 on a device. Stories: US1.
